Aegis Civic Labs — Legal & Data Notice

1. Government Non-Affiliation & Public Sources

H2Know is developed and published independently by Aegis Civic Labs. H2Know does NOT represent, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by, the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS), or any state or municipal water utility.

All water system identifiers (PWSID), violation histories, lead/copper percentiles, and drought indices are retrieved from official public-domain federal databases:

EPA SDWIS Database U.S. Drought Monitor

2. Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

• Anonymous ZIP Searches: You can search any U.S. ZIP code and inspect all 4W water reports without signing in. Entered ZIP codes are sent to public EPA Envirofacts, Zippopotam.us, Census ZCTA, and EPA service-area boundary endpoints to resolve local utilities.

• Optional Google Sign-In & Cloud Sync: If you choose to sign in with Google, H2Know stores your saved ZIP history snapshots and community-reported water issues in Google Firebase Firestore.

• Instant Data Deletion & Zero Ad Tracking: H2Know contains zero third-party ad networks and never sells user data. Signed-in users can permanently delete any saved ZIP history snapshot or reported issue at any time using the trash icon next to the item.

3. Informational & Health Disclaimer

H2Know scores and contaminant benchmarks are provided for educational and civic transparency purposes only and do not constitute medical advice or certified laboratory certification for individual tap plumbing. For certified lead or chemical testing at your tap, contact an EPA-accredited drinking water laboratory.
