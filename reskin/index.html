Privacy and EPA notice

Not a government site

H2Know is published independently by Aegis Civic Labs. It does not represent, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by, the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, the U.S. Geological Survey, or any state or municipal water utility.

System IDs, violation history, lead and copper samples, and drought status come from public federal sources: the EPA SDWIS database and the U.S. Drought Monitor.

What is stored

ZIP lookups work without an account. A ZIP is sent only to public EPA Envirofacts and Zippopotam endpoints to find the local utility.

On the web, saved lookups and notes stay in this browser. You can delete them. The Android app can sync the same kind of note with Firebase when you sign in there. H2Know does not run ads and does not sell user data.

Not a lab test

Scores and filter notes are for civic context. They are not medical advice and they are not a certified test of the plumbing in your home. For a certified test, use an EPA-accredited drinking water laboratory.
